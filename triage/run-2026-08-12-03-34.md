## Key Observations

1. **This batch is overwhelmingly recon/novelty noise.** 21 of 25 leads fail immediately — mostly discovery docs, already-fixed commits, published CVEs/advisories, and "endpoint exists" observations.

2. **No lead crosses all 7 questions cleanly.** The three HOLDs (4, 8, 21) have *potential* but require confirmation of: (a) scope (is it against a bounty-covered service, not just open-source SDK?), (b) reachability (unauthenticated or cross-tenant?), and (c) current unfixed status.

3. **The hunt bot is operating at "API surface enumeration" depth.** It's finding discovery docs, GitHub issues, public research, and theoretical attack classes — not live exploitable vulnerabilities. The signal-to-noise ratio is ~0%.

4. **Recommendation:** The bot's pipeline needs tightening before producing leads for human triage. Specifically: filter out `$discovery/rest` documents (always public), filter out merged/fixed commits and CVE-numbered items, and require at least one of: unauthorized access proof, cross-tenant data exposure, or active exploitable condition before emitting a lead.
