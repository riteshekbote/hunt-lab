## Recommendation

**Escalate zero leads.** The hunt run produced only enumeration noise, duplicates of public issues, and patch-recycling. The bot is:
1. Mistaking discovery documents and JWKS endpoints for vulnerabilities.
2. Recycling open GitHub issues as "unvalidated" findings.
3. Reporting fix commits as if they were live vulnerabilities.
4. Recycling published CVEs and credited research as fresh findings.
5. Reporting POST-only endpoints despite the passive-only rule.

**Suggested tuning:** tighten the prompt to require (a) a demonstrable security impact beyond schema disclosure, (b) a dedup check against open issues/CVEs before emitting a lead, (c) explicit rejection of public-key/JWKS "findings" as non-vulns, (d) rejection of fix commits and PRs as findings, and (e) enforcement of the passive-only rule (no POST-only leads).
